Our story

Since 2002

The first in Lahore to sell BBQ by weight.

In 2002, BBQ Plus started on Sham Nagar Road with a simple idea: you should pay for exactly the BBQ you want. No fixed plates, no guessing. Ask for a pao, half a kg or a full kilo, and we weigh it fresh.

More than twenty years later the charcoal is still lit every evening, and Malai Boti is still the reason people line up.
